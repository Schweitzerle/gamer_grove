# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 July 2026

This policy explains which personal data the GamerGrove app processes, for what
purpose and on what legal basis. The German version is the legally binding one.

## 2. What we process

### Account

When you register we process your email address and a password you choose. The
password is stored only as a cryptographic hash; we cannot read it.

Your profile additionally holds: username, optional display name, optional bio,
optional avatar and optional country. Those are voluntary.

### Content you create

Game ratings, wishlist, recommendations, your Top 3, custom collections and the
users you follow. This data is linked to your account. The visibility settings
in your profile control what other users can see.

### Technical data

Connecting to our servers and to the services listed below transmits
technically necessary data, in particular your IP address.

## 3. Purposes and legal bases

- Providing the account and app features: performance of the contract,
  Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.
- Handling subscriptions: performance of the contract, Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.
- Diagnostics, stability and anonymous usage measurement: legitimate interest
  in a working and improving app, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR.

## 4. Recipients and services

- Supabase (database, authentication, file storage). Holds your account and
  content. Server location: Frankfurt, Germany (EU).
- IGDB / Twitch (Internet Games Database) for game data. Fetching game
  information transmits your IP address to that service. No account data is
  sent. Provider: Twitch Interactive, Inc., USA.
- Sentry (crash and error reports). Transmission of personal data is disabled
  (no IP, no user identifier); we receive technical data such as stack trace,
  app version and device type. Server location: Germany (Sentry's EU region).
- Umami (usage measurement, self-hosted by us). Records event names only, with
  no user identifier and no cookies. No cross-device recognition takes place.
  Server location: Nuremberg, Germany (Hetzner Cloud).
- RevenueCat (subscription management). Receives an identifier of your account
  so a purchased subscription can be attributed to you. Provider: RevenueCat,
  Inc., USA.
- Google Play Billing (payment processing). The purchase runs entirely through
  Google; we never receive payment details such as card numbers. Provider:
  Google Ireland Limited.

Our own infrastructure — database, error reports and usage measurement — is
entirely in the EU. Data goes to the USA only for IGDB / Twitch (your IP address
when game data is fetched) and RevenueCat (an account identifier so a
subscription can be attributed). Those transfers are based on the EU
Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses or the recipient's certification under
the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

## 5. Retention

We keep account data and content for as long as your account exists. If you
delete your account, your profile, ratings, wishlist, recommendations, Top 3,
collections, follow relationships and your credentials are deleted. Error
reports and usage data carry no account reference and are deleted independently
after a short period.

## 6. Deleting your account

You can delete your account yourself at any time: in the app under Profile, at
the bottom, via "Delete account". Deletion is immediate and permanent.

This does not cancel an active subscription. Subscriptions are managed by
Google — cancel yours in the Google Play Store before deleting.

## 7. Your rights

You have the right of access (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16), erasure
(Art. 17), restriction (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and to object to
processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 21). Contact us at the email
address above.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority, in
particular in the member state where you live.

## 8. Children

The app is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect data
from anyone under 16.

## 9. Changes

We update this policy when the app or the services it uses change. The current
version is always available in the app.
